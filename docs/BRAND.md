# Tillward

Selected by the owner on 2026-10-22. Former working name: Paydown. Built by ElseMade.

Tillward gives a BTC-holding business working capital, then uses its chosen share of MUSD customer receipts to reduce native Mezo debt. The cash floor leaves room for operations. The identity should feel like a tool used at the business counter.

## Directions considered

1. **Receipt counter / Tillward — selected.** A counter-shaped lowercase t holds a separate hanging receipt. The counter stays in place as receipts arrive. Ink, cream and vermilion; editorial headings and plain operational numbers. Main risk: becoming a generic point-of-sale identity. The separate receipt geometry and retained horizontal counter provide a product-specific motif.
2. **Ledger / Paydown.** Open ledger, paired entries and a direct descriptive name. Bone, graphite and ledger green; tabular typography. Clear financial meaning, but too close to bookkeeping tools and repayment trackers.
3. **Measured room.** A fixed baseline with a movable allocation divider: operating space remains while debt reduces. Chalk, aubergine and ochre; condensed labels. Strong interaction language, weaker logo silhouette and a risk of looking like analytics software.

The selected mark reads as a counter and receipt in monochrome, has no arrows/currency/shields/chain decoration, and remains legible at favicon size. Its short structural description is “counter t with a hanging receipt.” Small-size and monochrome variants live in public/brand. An initial exact-name web search did not surface an obvious Tillward result; this is not trademark or domain clearance. No domain was purchased.

## System

- Ink #24251f: text, mark and structure.
- Cream #f6f2e9: workspace; #fffdf8 for work surfaces.
- Vermilion #b53b24: primary actions and allocation emphasis.
- Newsreader: headings/wordmark; DM Sans: operational text and numeric data.
- Keep semantic green for confirmed financial states. Color is never the only state signal.
- Prefer a fixed counter line, a receipt edge and purposeful spacing over decorative gradients.
- Positioning: “BTC-backed working capital. Repay as receipts arrive.”
