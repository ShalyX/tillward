## Eligibility and rules

Every entry must integrate MUSD and/or MEZO, have a working deployment, and add utility to the ecosystem. Mezo, Ethereum, Base and BNB Chain are supported; Mezo testnet is explicitly accepted. New products and new integrations/extensions to existing products can enter. Reused code must be open source and credited. Wave 2 entries must identify meaningful progress after Wave 1.

Entrants must be at least 18, join no more than one team per Wave, and complete individual AKINDO registration. The overview also requires joining Mezo Discord and accepting participation terms. Employees of Supernormal or involved subcontractors and their relatives are excluded. Geographic restrictions include DPRK, DRC, Cuba, Donetsk, Iran, Iraq, Luhansk, Crimea, mainland China and Syria, plus applicable sanctions/embargo and local-law restrictions. These are organizer restrictions, not an eligibility determination for this owner.

Registration asks for name, email, Telegram/Discord/GitHub, experience, focus, existing/new product, location and continuation intent. Owner must supply or confirm these and personally handle legal declarations. No identity, age, jurisdiction, employment or agreement response has been invented.

The rules grant contest/promotional display rights while retaining entrant IP, include name/likeness promotional consent, place taxes on recipients, and provide BVI governing law/dispute jurisdiction. The owner must review the linked participation terms before agreement. Reward recipients complete Mezo KYB. Awards are proportional to judging points and paid in MEZO tokens using the stated 14-day TWAP mechanism, not guaranteed cash prizes.

## Required submission package

- Live testnet or mainnet project URL.
- Public GitHub repository.
- Product/project deck.
- Two-minute pitch video.
- Completed AKINDO submission details.

The overview lists these form details: project name; category in 3–5 words; one-sentence TL;DR; problem, workflow and MUSD/MEZO role; target users including experience/region/device; tech stack; Track 1, Track 2 or both; deployed chain; new project or extension; mainnet/testnet/demo readiness; next three milestones with timelines if continuing; members, roles and X or LinkedIn. Wave 2 resubmissions describe the changes since Wave 1.

| Technical Implementation | 20% |

The live organizer comment says judges expect working features that users can test and no clear security flaws. It also states code can improve until the deadline. See [SCORECARD.md](SCORECARD.md) for the evidence and remaining gaps.

## Submission gates

All links must work publicly without the builder's session. Identify Mezo testnet clearly and use only test assets. Preserve historical block numbers for recorded amounts rather than claiming current customer revenue. The deck/video must show the user workflow and actual native debt reduction. The owner still needs to confirm registration, team/contact details, eligibility and terms, and the final submission must have a visible AKINDO receipt.
