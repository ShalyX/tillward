# Tillward design lock

The owner selected the receipt-counter identity on October 22. BRAND.md is canonical for the visual system.

Ink #24251f, cream #f6f2e9, vermilion #b53b24. Newsreader editorial headings and DM Sans operational text. A counter-shaped t with a hanging receipt anchors the mark. The identity should feel like a tool used at a business counter.

Product screens lead with available cash, actual debt, customer receipts and the owner's repayment rule. Status colors always have text labels. Example workspaces, failed reads and live fixed-block state are visibly distinct.

Submission materials inherit the same type and palette, use real product captures, and show receipt allocation as a causal split. Native debt reduction is evidenced with recorded chain results rather than a fabricated transaction animation.

Avoid generic crypto shields, circuits, chain decoration, gradients, fake dashboards and claims of protecting BTC against liquidation. Motion serves allocation, progression and framing, not decoration. No new identity direction is introduced during implementation.
