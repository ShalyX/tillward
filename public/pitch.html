The pitch uses actual product captures and recorded testnet transactions. Illustrative numbers are labeled. The recorded original receipt was 500 MUSD, with 100 MUSD applied to native Mezo debt. Later recorded totals reached 502 MUSD collected and 100.4 MUSD repaid.

These are integration proofs funded with test assets. They do not represent customer revenue or adoption. Native collateral remains subject to liquidation and redemption. Tillward has no mainnet deployment or independent security audit.

The public demo needs no login. Its default workspace is illustrative. The real business link reads onchain state without a wallet, with explicit retry states for RPC failures. Owner actions still require the owning wallet.
