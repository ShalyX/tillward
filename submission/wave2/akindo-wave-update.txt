Tillward connects BTC-backed working capital to customer receipts. A business borrows MUSD, chooses its repayment share and collects final-payment invoices. Eligible receipts repay that same native Mezo loan.

Since Wave 1:
• Added native BTC price, ICR/TCR, Recovery Mode and owner collateral actions, including stale/unavailable states.
• Verified factory and account source on the explorer.
• Proved a public 0.001 test BTC collateral top-up and full owner exit.
• Completed a real native surplus claim: controlled redemption of only our borrower created 0.005532212424520610 test BTC. Tillward claimed it and the owner separately withdrew it.
• Deployed the selected 1% atomic routing fee in separate V2 contracts. A 500 test MUSD receipt at 99% allocation repaid 495 MUSD, paid 4.95 MUSD to the test treasury and left 0.05 MUSD. The lender received the full repayment. No deferred fees. V1 stays fee-free.
• Verified owner opt-out, 9.9 MUSD manual repayment without a new service fee, closure and 0.045 test BTC recovery.
• Simplified repayment rules and loan management; removed the reserve editor and extra settings tab.
• Extended monitoring and encrypted backups to both keepers, tested Telegram alert/recovery and restored V2 runtime in an isolated network namespace.

The fee is 1% of actual hosted receipt-funded repayment, from retained funds, with explicit owner enrollment. The maximum share is 99%. Native protocol/network fees are separate.

All figures are testnet QA with recycled test assets. Seller/payer acceptance and willingness to pay remain unvalidated; interviews are held. Monitoring needs the workstation online; a second-host restore remains open.
