Narrator: previously approved Emma neural voice. Finalized Wave 2 film script, November 6, 2026. The published 120-second film passed encoded visual/audio QA. Its labeled V2 recordings are preserved; the latest V3 results appear in the deck and judge guide.

## 000–010s · purpose

Your business holds Bitcoin, but needs working capital. With Tillward, you borrow against that Bitcoin and repay from customer receipts.

## 010–025s · borrow

Borrow MUSD against Bitcoin through Mezo. Check the native price, collateral ratios and Recovery Mode. Add collateral when you choose.

## 025–039s · rule

Choose your repayment share, up to ninety-nine percent. Enable automation when you're ready. Tillward charges one percent of the amount actually repaid.

## 039–054s · invoice

Create an invoice and share the payment link. Your customer approves the exact MUSD amount, then pays. The receipt and its repayment allocation are recorded in your business account.

## 054–069s · split

At twenty percent, a five-hundred MUSD payment repays one hundred. The service fee is one MUSD. Three hundred ninety-nine stays with the business.

## 069–086s · proof

The public test used the ninety-nine percent cap. Five hundred MUSD came in: four hundred ninety-five repaid, four point nine five paid the fee, and zero point zero five remained. In one transaction.

## 086–101s · exit

You can pause automation and still manage your loan. We verified manual repayment without another service fee, then closure and the return of zero point zero four five test Bitcoin.
